Characters

Your own characters: the ones the overlay has seen as the player, or found in D2R's save files (other players of a game are never listed). Pick the playstyle and build chain each one follows (saved at once; "class default": the playstyle with a default build for its class). The shared stash keeps what every character wants. Delete forgets a character here (its build selection and last-played time; D2R's save files are not touched; playing it again brings it back).

Money farming

mark wands, scepters, sorceress orbs and daggers that sell well to vendors

Off: no ring, coin or sound for them on the map, and the stash plan sells them like any other item (~/.local/share/d2r-map-overlay/settings.toml; the overlay applies it within seconds).

Not needed items

Every piece marked not needed (the ✕ on the live dashboard), by the build it was marked for, also when the build or the name matches nothing any more (~/.local/share/d2r-map-overlay/not-needed.toml). Bring back makes it wanted again.

Loot filter

Each playstyle's D2R loot filter profile (loot-filter.json; for the selected playstyle a copy in ~/.config/d2r-map-overlay/playstyle/ overrides it). Copy it, then import it in the game.
